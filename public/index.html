About Me

I'm Isaac, a VFX artist and motion designer who lives at the intersection of technical simulation and musical storytelling.

In Houdini, I build destruction sequences, fluid sims, pyro effects, and procedural systems that push what's visually possible. Every node has a purpose.

In After Effects, I create lyrical animations that make words dance — synced to rhythm, breathing with the beat, turning music into a visual experience.

I believe great VFX isn't just about technical precision — it's about feeling something.
